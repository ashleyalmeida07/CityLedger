How CityLedger Works

A transparent, AI-powered, blockchain-verified civic complaint management system that connects citizens with their municipal corporations

The Complete Process

1

Citizen Reports an Issue

Citizens file complaints through an intuitive guided wizard that captures:

  • Photos/videos of the issue
  • GPS location (auto-captured)
  • Detailed description through smart questions
  • Contact information
2

AI Analysis & Classification

NVIDIA Llama 3.3 70B AI model instantly analyzes the complaint:

  • Detects category (Pothole, Street Lamp, Garbage, Water Leak, etc.)
  • Assigns severity level (LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, CRITICAL)
  • Identifies duplicates to prevent redundancy
  • Generates summary for officers
3

Blockchain Verification

The complaint is recorded on Ethereum blockchain:

  • Immutable proof of submission
  • Tamper-proof timestamp
  • Public verification via Etherscan
  • Ensures accountability and transparency
4

Officer Triage & Assignment

Municipal officers review and assign complaints:

  • View AI-generated summaries and recommendations
  • Prioritize based on severity and SLA timers
  • Assign to appropriate field workers
  • Monitor queue and track progress
5

Field Worker Resolution

Field workers receive tasks and resolve issues:

  • GPS navigation to complaint location
  • Real-time status updates
  • Upload completion photos
  • AI validates work quality (before/after comparison)
6

Citizen Tracking & Feedback

Citizens stay informed throughout the process:

  • Real-time status updates
  • Timeline view of progress
  • View completion photos
  • Upvote similar issues to escalate priority
